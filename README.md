## About

I am an Information Systems student and Full Stack / Software Engineer based in Campina Grande, Brazil. I work across product engineering, automation and applied AI—from user experience and data modeling to backend reliability, asynchronous processing and controlled execution.

I am especially interested in systems that stay understandable as they grow: explicit boundaries, deterministic fallbacks, observable decisions and AI that earns its place in the architecture.

## How I build

- Treat frontend, backend, data and operations as one product system.
- Prefer explicit control boundaries over opaque automation.
- Keep important decisions observable, testable and auditable.
- Use AI where it improves the product—not where a deterministic rule is better.
